Lesson 1

Foundations — Heisig frames 1–20

Twenty foundational characters from 一 to 明: numbers, sun and moon, field and eye, then early compounds of meaning (old, I, risk, companion, bright). Each character carries a Japanese Reflection verse and study image.

Status: draft · lesson_001 · 20 kanji · 143 vocab · 156 phrases · 89 compounds · YouTube: unpublished

Kanji

  1. 一 one

    H1 · イチ · 1 strokes

    A single persimmon glowed upon the wooden floor, while afternoon light through the shoji held the room in stillness.

  2. 二 two

    H2 · ニ · 2 strokes

    Two lanterns drifted above the temple at night, while only the gentle darkness swayed.

  3. 三 three

    H3 · サン · 3 strokes

    Three persimmons rested upon the dark tray, and beside the folded fan, autumn quietly breathed.

  4. 四 four

    H4 · シ · 5 strokes

    Four persimmons lined up upon the tray, while maple shadows on the shoji wrapped the autumn room.

  5. 五 five

    H5 · ゴ · 4 strokes

    Five carp streamed in the wind above the river, while only the water mirrored the spring.

  6. 六 six

    H6 · ロク · 4 strokes

    Steam rose from six tea bowls in a row, while the quiet seats waited for their guests.

  7. 七 seven

    H7 · シチ · 2 strokes

    Seven koi glided through the moonlit pond, while one alone turned toward the moon.

  8. 八 eight

    H8 · ハチ · 2 strokes

    At the roots of the great tree the path split in two, and the traveler alone paused before the choice.

  9. 九 nine

    H9 · キュウ · 2 strokes

    Lights continuing across the wet bridge.

  10. 十 ten

    H10 · ジュウ · 2 strokes

    At the morning market he folded indigo cloth, and the crossing of ten lay quietly woven in the fabric.

  11. 口 mouth

    H11 · コウ · 3 strokes

    Holding the steaming bowl in both hands, she opened her mouth and laughter filled the room.

  12. 日 sun

    H12 · ニチ · 4 strokes

    Morning sun poured across the terraced fields, while only the water burned with gold.

  13. 月 moon

    H13 · ゲツ · 4 strokes

    A fisherman mended his net upon the boat, while moonlight alone quietly kept him company.

  14. 田 field

    H14 · デン · 5 strokes

    The grid of paddies mirrored the morning sun, while only the misty valley quietly held them.

  15. 目 eye

    H15 · モク · 5 strokes

    Upon the bridge he watched the lanterns drift, while only the boy's eyes remembered the night.

  16. 古 old

    H16 · コ · 5 strokes

    Morning light touched the trunk of the ancient cedar, which quietly carried the prayers of generations.

  17. 吾 I

    H17 · ゴ · 7 strokes

  18. 冒 risk

    H18 · ボウ · 9 strokes

    Upon the snowy ridge they clasped reaching hands, and one unseen step bound them together.

  19. 朋 companion

    H19 · ホウ · 8 strokes

    Facing each other across the snowy garden, only the warmth of the brazier joined them together.

  20. 明 bright

    H20 · メイ · 8 strokes

    The hearth fire lit their faces with warmth, and only their bright laughter lingered in the room.
