唱 chant

Lesson 2

Foundations — Heisig frames 21–40

Twenty characters from 唱 to 千 (Heisig 21–40), continuing Book 1 foundations with Japanese Reflection verses and study imagery.

Status: draft · lesson_002 · 20 kanji · 152 vocab · 170 phrases · 87 compounds · YouTube: unpublished

Kanji

  1. 唱 chant

    H21 · ショウ · 11 strokes

    In the belfry at dusk the temple bell hung still, while only the quiet light wrapped around its voice.

  2. 晶 sparkle

    H22 · ショウ · 12 strokes

    Three crystals rested upon the wooden table, while only their faceted light spilled toward the forest.

  3. 品 goods

    H23 · ヒン · 9 strokes

    Three bowls were stacked upon the table, while only the shoji light warmed their metal.

  4. 呂 spine

    H24 · ロ · 7 strokes

    Two bowls aligned in midair one above the other, while only the vertical gap between them held their form.

  5. 昌 prosperous

    H25 · ショウ · 8 strokes

    Two suns rested quietly upon the lake, while only the misty valley held their light.

  6. 早 early

    H26 · ソウ · 6 strokes

    Upon the morning plain a single post stood tall, while only the cross of water heralded the day.

  7. 旭 rising_sun

    H27 · キョク · 6 strokes

    From behind the mountain's edge the sun appeared, while only the post in the pond kept its shadow.

  8. 世 generation

    H28 · セイ · 5 strokes

    For countless generations the same lake was lit, while only the golden mist wrapped the shoreline.

  9. 胃 stomach

    H29 · イ · 9 strokes

    In the quiet room light overflowed from the bowl, while only the evening mountains continued beyond the window.

  10. 旦 dawn

    H30 · タン · 5 strokes

    From the horizon the morning sun showed its face, while only the open sky welcomed the light.

  11. 胆 gallbladder

    H31 · タン · 9 strokes

    Above the bowl a single drop hung motionless, while only its golden light filled the room.

  12. 亘 span

    H32 · コウ · 6 strokes

    Through the evening valley the river curved wide, while only the band of light continued across the mountains.

  13. 凹 concave

    H33 · オウ · 5 strokes

    The crescent bowl glowed upon the wooden table, while only the sunset through the window mirrored its hollow.

  14. 凸 convex

    H34 · トツ · 5 strokes

    The boat-shaped bowl swelled upon the table, while only the light within pushed the shadow upward.

  15. 旧 olden_times

    H35 · キュウ · 5 strokes

    Sunset light streamed through the aged window, while only the worn walls kept the passing years.

  16. 自 oneself

    H36 · ジ · 6 strokes

    Upon the ledge by the window a glowing sphere rested, and only a solitary stillness remained in the room.

  17. 白 white

    H37 · ハク · 5 strokes

    A bouquet of white flowers rested by the window, while only the sunset stained the petals.

  18. 百 hundred

    H38 · ヒャク · 6 strokes

    Origami cranes overflowed the wooden tray, while only the evening light lit a hundred wings.

  19. 中 middle

    H39 · チュウ · 4 strokes

    Light passed through the center of the glowing sphere, while only a vertical line held the stillness.

  20. 千 thousand

    H40 · セン · 3 strokes

    From the corridor ceiling a thousand cranes hung down, while only the endless light continued toward the back.
