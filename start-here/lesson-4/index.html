Room 4 — Reading names

Phrases you can already say, now with a few new hiragana.

Reading

おなまえ

お and え you already have. First notice な, then ま.

なんですか

な is no longer new. ん is.

は in はい is *ha*. In おなまえは it is often *wa*. Same box, two jobs.

さん

ん you just met. さ is the new piece.

こんにちは

ん and は you know. The new star is こ. に and ち can wait.

よろしく still as a whole — except し, which you already met in たのしい.
