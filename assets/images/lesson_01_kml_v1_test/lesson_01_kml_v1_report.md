### Strongest 5 (post-generation visual review)

1. **明 bright** — KML-GLOW accent. Dual sun/moon gorge with integrated glowing 明; warm/cool paired radiance; human observer with visible profile; lesson climax energy; exhibition-ready scale.
2. **月 moon** — KML-WASH. Crescent over still rice paddy with reflected 月 kanji as stone monument; perfect palette wash; wide atmospheric; no humans needed.
3. **日 sun** — KML-WASH. Low sun through horizontal cloud bands; integrated calligraphic 日; wide field horizon; immediate keyword read without feeling illustrative.
4. **冒 risk** — KML-CINE accent. Figure stepping across dark water gap; glowing carved 冒; face visible in profile; tension matches accent reason; strongest human-presence execution.
5. **朋 companion** — KML-WASH. Two travelers side-by-side on misty forest path; both faces visible; integrated 朋 in fog; relational read is immediate and cinematic.

**Honorable mentions:** 田 field (terraced grid mirrors 田 shape), 口 mouth (cave + square opening), 十 ten (crossroads stone cross), 八 eight (forking path mirrors 八 strokes), 古 old (gnarled cedar with integrated 古).

### Weakest 5 (post-generation visual review)

1. **一 one** — Cross-shaped pole in mist reads as 十 (ten), not 一 (single horizontal stroke). Scene brief (single reed) was ignored; highest misrecognition risk in the set.
2. **六 six** — Six-petal flower in bowl is accurate but reads as still-life/product shot rather than wide cinematic ambient; scale too tight for exhibition loops.
3. **七 seven** — Bent branch/path is poetic but weak numeric signal without the floating 七 kanji; landscape alone could mean “path” or “turn.”
4. **九 nine** — Nine-stone circle works symbolically, but kanji placed upper-right feels label-like rather than environmental integration.
5. **目 eye** — Eye-shaped pool + reflected 目 is strong symbolically, but distant figure is a silhouette (face not visible) — violates human-presence rule.

**Also watch:** 二 two, 三 three, 五 five, 八 eight — rely heavily on prominent floating kanji for keyword recognition; scenic elements alone would be ambiguous. 吾 I and 明 bright include kanji as mental projection, which works but blurs the “no text” boundary.

### Recurring issues

| Issue | Affected keywords | Notes |
|-------|-------------------|-------|
| Floating kanji as primary identifier | two, three, five, seven, eight, nine, most numbers | Symbolic scenery secondary; kanji often flat overlay not sculptural |
| Number stroke-shape confusion | one (→ ten), four vs field (both square grids) | Need explicit stroke-count geometry in scene briefs |
| Kanji-as-label vs integrated form | nine, two, seven | Corner/center floating characters feel educational |
| Human face requirement missed | eye (silhouette) | KML-WASH human_presence defaults too passive for eye/I prompts |
| Tight vs wide framing drift | six, old | Some medium shots work; six bowl shot too close for ambient gallery |
| “No text” vs optional kanji tension | most of set | Prompt allows optional sculptural kanji; model defaults to visible floating character |
| Palette consistency | all 20 | KML-WASH ivory/grey-blue/ochre held well across set |
| Accent styles | risk (CINE), bright (GLOW) | Both accent treatments clearly differentiated from dominant WASH |

### Recommendations for lesson_visual_styles_v1.json

1. **Add `style_profiles` block** — Move composition, lighting, atmosphere, human_presence, symbolic_treatment, image_distance, surface, noir_alignment from script into JSON per style code (KML-WASH, KML-GLOW, KML-CINE).
2. **Add `number_keyword_rules`** — For lesson 1 digits: require stroke-shape geometry (一 = single horizontal mark, not cross; 十 = cross only) and forbid ambiguous forms shared between numerals.
3. **Add `kanji_integration` policy** — Distinguish `environmental` (carved stone, light-form, mist) vs `forbidden` (floating overlay, corner label). Current “optional sculptural” is interpreted too loosely.
4. **Add per-kanji overrides** — `scene_brief`, `image_distance`, `human_presence`, `kanji_integration` per slug; e.g. eye/I require `face_visible: true`.
5. **Add shared `anti_patterns`** — No floating typography, no corner labels, no still-life product framing, no silhouettes when humans required, no English, no stock-photo lighting.
6. **Document accent merge rules** — How lesson palette inherits into GLOW/CINE accents (risk kept ochre rim; bright amplified dual-source glow).
7. **Add `recognition_test` field** — Per keyword: what visual element must be identifiable without kanji overlay (for QA scoring).
8. **Split number vs primitive treatment** — Numbers 1–10 may need a dedicated sub-style (KML-STROKE) with close framing and explicit count geometry, separate from wide atmospheric primitives (sun, moon, field).
