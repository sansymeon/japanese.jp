漢検への道

漢検10級から2級まで、常用漢字2,136字を級ごとに学びます。字数は、その級で新しく学ぶ漢字の数です。

Kanji Proficiency

Level 10 is the beginner level. Level 2 is the advanced end of this path. This is the Japanese study system. After you choose a level, the lessons and character lists are primarily in Japanese.
