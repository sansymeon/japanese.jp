KML Component Layout Templates (Phase 1)

Goal: Only horizontal and vertical layouts. More complex structures are
built by nesting these primitives.

-   Avoid enclosure-specific layouts.
-   Keep the renderer simple.
-   Lessons 1–40 will define the editorial style.
