Hard cases and editorial decisions

諦 — 諦念 is a formal but still useful N1-level noun; everyday alternatives are mostly inflections of the Volume 1 anchor 諦める.
瞳, 滝, 芯 — these kanji have no comparably common distinct two-kanji compound; the standalone lexical forms are retained for useful reading coverage.
諧, 褐, 憬, 抄, 拓, 拐 — modern everyday alternatives are limited. Selections favor the most recognizable available word, even when formal, literary, specialist, or less frequent.
肪 — almost all productive uses occur inside 脂肪; 体脂肪 adds an everyday health/fitness context without duplicating the exact Volume 1 anchor.
蛍 — 蛍光 is the productive core of the Volume 1 term 蛍光灯 and provides a concise, distinct vocabulary item.
錮 — 禁錮刑 is the standard legal expansion of 禁錮 and was chosen because independent alternatives are effectively unavailable.
恣, 憾 — 恣意的 and 遺憾ながら are high-frequency formal extensions of their respective Volume 1 anchors; there is no broadly useful independent alternative.
憬 — 憧憬 is effectively the only modern lexical item containing 憬 and is already a Volume 1 anchor for 憬; retained as an unavoidable exception.
拉 — 拉致問題 is a current-affairs extension of the Volume 1 anchor 拉致; independent alternatives are not practical.
搭 — 搭乗券 was chosen over Volume 1 anchors 搭乗 and 搭載; it is a common travel term.
拶 — 挨拶状 is the natural productive extension of the only common word containing 拶.
