Vol. 2 batch 06 curation notes
===============================

Count: 135 entries, in the same order as vol1_batch_06.json.
Validation: every target kanji occurs in its selected compound; no selected
compound exactly duplicates its Volume 1 anchor.

Uncertain / constrained cases
-----------------------------
- 氾 → 氾濫原: the character has virtually no broadly everyday standalone
  compound beyond the Volume 1 anchor 氾濫. This is the common geography term
  “floodplain.”
- 臼 → 臼状: a standard anatomical/descriptive word, but technical. Everyday
  alternatives are scarce once 臼歯 is excluded.
- 毀 → 毀誉: a literary/book-register pairing (“praise and blame”), selected
  as the most established non-anchor option.
- 濫 → 粗製濫造: an established newspaper/book idiom, rather than an everyday
  conversation word; usable vocabulary options outside 濫用 are very limited.
- 璃 → 瑠璃製: rare character with little productive modern vocabulary; this
  is a transparent descriptive compound and remains a low-frequency item.
- 摯 → 摯友: established but bookish (“close friend”); the kanji is itself
  rare outside 真摯.
- 睦 → 睦月: a culturally useful traditional name for January, although
  limited to calendar/literary contexts.
- 劾 → 糾劾: formal newspaper/legal-register word; substantially more useful
  than archaic alternatives, but still uncommon outside political reporting.
- 壌 → 壌土: common enough in soil/gardening contexts, but specialist relative
  to the excluded 土壌.
- 泰 → 泰然: standard formal expression, mainly book/news register.
- 奉 → 奉る: a natural honorific verb, but formal and less frequent than
  奉仕.
- 僅 → 僅少: formal written-register alternative to 僅か.
- 吟 → 吟詠: familiar in the context of poetry recitation, but a cultural
  term rather than everyday speech.
- 琴 → 琴柱: the native-reading term for a koto bridge; constrained because
  琴 is mostly limited to instrument vocabulary.
- 慄 → 慄然: a book-register word. Common modern alternatives reuse the
  Volume 1 anchor 戦慄.
- 扉 → 扉絵: a publishing/book term, selected over the Volume 1 gate-door
  sense.

Intentional reading/sense diversification
------------------------------------------
- 酬いる, 酔う, 塞ぐ, 避ける, 漬ける, 醒める, 貪る, and 奉る add natural
  kun-readings or a distinct verb use.
- 柳葉魚 (ししゃも), 餌やり, 餅つき, 薪ストーブ, and 喉仏 provide common
  native-reading or mixed-script everyday vocabulary.
- 瑠璃色, 鑑賞, 連峰, 韓流, 経緯, and 当該 broaden the anchor sense/reading
  with useful cultural, book, or news vocabulary.
