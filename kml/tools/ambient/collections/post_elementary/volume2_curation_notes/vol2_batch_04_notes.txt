Volume 2 curation notes — batch 04

Selection policy
- Each entry retains the source kanji, Heisig number, slug, and display order.
- Every selected anchor contains its target kanji and differs from its Volume 1 anchor.
- Ruby supplies one complete surface/reading pair per anchor.

Cases with limited natural alternatives
- 糧: 精神的糧 is an established, literary-leaning but still productive expression; standalone compounds are limited beyond 食糧.
- 菊: 白菊 is a familiar everyday flower term, but 菊 has few broadly useful non-anchor compounds.
- 伎: 歌舞伎座 is a proper venue name and extends the otherwise nearly fixed 歌舞伎 usage.
- 傲: 傲慢さ is the natural nominal extension of 傲慢; independent alternatives are literary.
- 仙: 仙台 is a high-frequency place name; non-proper-name compounds for 仙 are generally literary or niche.
- 畝: 畝幅 is standard agricultural vocabulary, but this kanji has no genuinely everyday compound.
- 丙: 丙午 is a culturally recognizable calendar term, though less frequent than the anchor.
- 賜: 賜与 is formal/news-register vocabulary; natural modern alternatives are limited.
- 祉: 社会福祉 extends the Vol. 1 item with a high-frequency public-policy term; 祉 otherwise occurs almost exclusively in 福祉.
- 款: 借款 is established finance/news vocabulary, but more formal than the usual target level.
- 岬: 岬巡り is a transparent travel expression rather than a fixed dictionary headword; compounds containing 岬 are sparse.
- 斤: 斤売り is a valid market/measure expression but is regionally and contextually limited.
- 斥: 斥候 is common in history, games, and fiction but is less everyday than most selections.
