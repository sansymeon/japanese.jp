Volume 2 curation notes

Low-productivity / constrained kanji:
- 呂: 呂律 is the most useful modern word beyond bath-related 風呂; it is common in the fixed expression 呂律が回らない.
- 升: 升席 is culturally current in sumo, but 升 has few broadly useful modern compounds.
- 乙: 乙種 is a standard category label (licenses, qualifications, and regulations), chosen over the Vol. 1 personal-noun sense.
- 肖: 不肖 is a formal humble expression; alternatives are mostly the excluded 肖像 family.
- 硝: 硝酸 is a high-frequency scientific/news compound, though specialized.
- 汎: 汎用性 intentionally extends, rather than duplicates, Vol. 1 汎用 and supplies the very common “versatility” usage.
- 汰 / 沙: Both use 沙汰, a useful common term (news, judgment, or consequence); each character has very few independent contemporary alternatives.
- 厘: 分厘 is retained as the least specialized real compound; 厘 is otherwise mostly encountered in fractional amounts.
- 涯, 宵, 冥, 坑, 享, 荘, 桟, 戚, 肯: selected the most usable non-anchor compounds available, though each is formal, literary, or context-limited.
- 喩: 暗喩 is the most useful distinct academic term after 比喩.
- 賂: 賄賂罪 necessarily retains the 賄賂 base because 賂 is overwhelmingly productive only in that word family; it adds common legal/news usage.
- 訃 and 詔: 訃告 and 詔勅 are legitimate alternatives but remain formal and limited in contemporary everyday language.
- 弐: 弐万円 preserves the formal anti-tampering numeral context while varying the amount; there is no broadly distinct modern compound for 弐.
- 堤: 堤岸 is a standard civil-engineering/river term, selected because the everyday core compound 堤防 is excluded.
