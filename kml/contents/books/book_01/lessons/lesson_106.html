媛 maiden

春はるの御ごて殿んの縁えん側がわで、
媛ひめは遠とおい山やまを静しずかに見みつめる。
華はなやかな衣ころもの内うちにも、誰だれにも見みえぬ願ねがいがある。

From the veranda of the spring palace,
a noble lady quietly gazes toward the distant mountains.
Even beneath splendid robes, unseen wishes remain.

On: エン ・ Kun: ひめ

旅たびび人とたちは黙だまって手てを貸かし合あう。
援たすける力ちからは、一ひと人りの重おもさを皆みなで持もつこと。

Before the overturned cart,
the travellers silently lend one another their hands.
To support someone is to share what one person cannot carry alone.

緩 slacken

職しょくに人んは鉢はち巻まきを緩ゆるめて空そらを見み上あげる。

At the end of a long day,
the craftsman loosens his headband and looks toward the sky.
Even a strained heart is given rest by evening.

昔むかしの友ともと向むかい合あう。

By chance, while sheltering from the rain at a teahouse,

Long years can fold themselves into a single coincidence.
