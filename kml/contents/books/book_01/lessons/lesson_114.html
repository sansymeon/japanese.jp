做 make do

山やまざ里との工こう房ぼうで、
職しょくに人んは黙だまって一ひとつの椀わんを做なす。
形かたちあるものは、静しずかな手てから生うまれる。

In a mountain workshop,
a craftsman silently makes a single bowl.
Things of form are born from quiet hands.

On a winter night, the moon shines clear,
casting pale light across the frozen fields.
The colder the sky, the purer the light appears.
