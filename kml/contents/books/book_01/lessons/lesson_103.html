麺 noodles

冬ふゆの店みせ先さきで麺めんをすすると、
白しろい湯ゆ気げが顔かおを包つつんでゆく。
一いち椀わんの温あたたかさが、旅たびの疲つかれをほどく。

Slurping noodles at a winter shop,
white steam gathers around the face.
The warmth of one bowl loosens the weariness of travel.

The strongest things often take time to become familiar.

Sound judgment is born from eyes that do not hurry.
