Compound Words – Lesson 4

見 (ケン / み・る、み・える、み・せる) – See
  • 見る【みる】– To see, to look at
  • 見える【みえる】– To be visible
  • 見せる【みせる】– To show
  • 意見【いけん】– Opinion, view
  • 見学【けんがく】– Observation, field trip
  • 発見【はっけん】– Discovery
児 (ジ / に) – Newborn Babe
  • 児童【じどう】– Child, juvenile
  • 幼児【ようじ】– Infant, toddler
  • 小児科【しょうにか】– Pediatrics
  • 孤児【こじ】– Orphan
  • 胎児【たいじ】– Fetus
  • 新生児【しんせいじ】– Newborn baby
元 (ゲン / ガン / もと) – Beginning
  • 元【もと】– Origin, base, former (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 元気【げんき】– Healthy, energetic
  • 元日【がんじつ】– New Year’s Day
  • 元祖【がんそ】– Founder, originator
  • 地元【じもと】– Local area, hometown
  • 元通り【もとどおり】– As before, back to original state
頁 (ケツ) – Page
  • 頁【ページ】– Page (often used as a counter; rarely on its own)
  • 頁数【けっすう】– Number of pages
  • 頭頁【とうけつ】– Front page (rare, literary use)
  • 最終頁【さいしゅうけつ】– Last page
  • 頁脚【けっきゃく】– Page footer (layout/design term)
  • 頁頭【けっとう】– Page header
頑 (ガン) – Stubborn
  • 頑固【がんこ】– Stubborn, obstinate
  • 頑張る【がんばる】– To do one’s best, to persist
  • 頑丈【がんじょう】– Solid, sturdy, robust
  • 頑迷【がんめい】– Pigheaded, narrow-minded
  • 頑健【がんけん】– Healthy and strong
  • 頑愚【がんぐ】– Stupidly stubborn (literary/rare)
凡 (ボン / ハン) – Mediocre
  • 凡人【ぼんじん】– Ordinary person, mediocrity
  • 平凡【へいぼん】– Commonplace, mediocre
  • 凡例【はんれい】– Usage example, explanatory note
  • 凡才【ぼんさい】– Average talent
  • 凡庸【ぼんよう】– Mediocrity, dullness
  • 非凡【ひぼん】– Extraordinary, uncommon (antonym)
負 (フ / ま・ける、ま・かす、お・う) – Defeat
  • 負ける【まける】– To lose (a match), to be defeated
  • 負かす【まかす】– To defeat, to beat (someone)
  • 負う【おう】– To bear, to carry (a burden)
  • 勝負【しょうぶ】– Match, contest (win or lose)
  • 背負う【せおう】– To carry on one's back, to take responsibility
  • 負傷【ふしょう】– Injury, wound (esp. in battle or sports)
万 (マン / バン) – Ten Thousand
  • 一万【いちまん】– Ten thousand
  • 万人【ばんにん】– Everyone, all people
  • 万歳【ばんざい】– Hooray! Long live! (celebratory shout)
  • 万全【ばんぜん】– Thorough, flawless, perfect
  • 万一【まんいち】– Just in case, by some chance
  • 万物【ばんぶつ】– All things in nature, everything in existence
句 (ク) – Phrase
  • 句【く】– Phrase, clause (grammar/poetry)
  • 文句【もんく】– Complaint; phrase, expression
  • 句読点【くとうてん】– Punctuation marks
  • 句集【くしゅう】– Haiku anthology
  • 熟句【じゅくく】– Idiomatic phrase (rare)
  • 警句【けいく】– Aphorism, maxim
肌 (キ / はだ) – Texture
  • 肌【はだ】– Skin, surface (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 肌触り【はだざわり】– Texture, how something feels to the touch
  • 肌色【はだいろ】– Skin color
  • 素肌【すはだ】– Bare skin, natural skin
  • 地肌【じはだ】– Natural texture, surface of the skin
  • 肌寒い【はだざむい】– Chilly, feeling a chill on the skin
旬 (ジュン / シュン) – Decameron (10-Day Period)
  • 上旬【じょうじゅん】– First third of the month (days 1–10)
  • 中旬【ちゅうじゅん】– Middle third of the month (days 11–20)
  • 下旬【げじゅん】– Last third of the month (days 21–end)
  • 旬【しゅん】– In season (for food, especially fish/vegetables)
  • 初旬【しょじゅん】– Early in the month (synonym for 上旬)
  • 旬報【じゅんぽう】– 10-day report, periodical update
勺 (シャク) – Ladle
  • 一勺【いっしゃく】– One shaku (traditional unit, ~18ml)
  • 勺【しゃく】– Traditional measure (now rarely used)
  • 酒勺【さかずき】– Sake ladle or cup (arch. usage)
  • 滴勺【てきしゃく】– Dropper, ladle for drops (rare term)
  • 毫勺【ごうしゃく】– Very small quantity (old compound)
  • 勺量【しゃくりょう】– Measuring by ladle/spoonful (arch. usage)
的 (テキ / まと) – Bull’s Eye
  • 的【まと】– Target, mark, bull’s eye
  • 目的【もくてき】– Purpose, goal
  • 具体的【ぐたいてき】– Concrete, specific
  • 標的【ひょうてき】– Target (literal or metaphorical)
  • 象徴的【しょうちょうてき】– Symbolic
  • 効果的【こうかてき】– Effective
首 (シュ / くび) – Neck
  • 首【くび】– Neck (Kunyomi standalone); also used for “head” or “fired”
  • 首都【しゅと】– Capital city
  • 首相【しゅしょう】– Prime Minister
  • 部首【ぶしゅ】– Radical (kanji component)
  • 選手【せんしゅ】– Athlete, player (literally "chosen neck")
  • 首輪【くびわ】– Collar (for pets, or symbolically for constraint)
乙 (オツ / おと) - Fish Guts
  • 乙【おつ】– Stylish; second in ranking; quirky taste
  • 乙女【おとめ】– Maiden, young lady
  • 甲乙【こうおつ】– A and B; superior and inferior
  • 乙種【おつしゅ】– Type B (e.g., license classification)
  • 乙案【おつあん】– Alternate plan (Plan B)
乱 (ラン / みだれる) - Riot
  • 乱【みだれ】– Disorder, turmoil
  • 混乱【こんらん】– Confusion, chaos
  • 暴乱【ぼうらん】– Riot, insurrection
  • 乱暴【らんぼう】– Violence, roughness
  • 乱用【らんよう】– Abuse, misuse
直 (チョク / なおす) - Straightaway
  • 直す【なおす】– To fix, to correct
  • 正直【しょうじき】– Honesty
  • 直接【ちょくせつ】– Direct, immediate
  • 直行【ちょっこう】– Going straight (non-stop)
  • 直前【ちょくぜん】– Just before
具 (グ) - Tool
  • 道具【どうぐ】– Tool, instrument
  • 家具【かぐ】– Furniture
  • 具合【ぐあい】– Condition, health
  • 具現化【ぐげんか】– Realization, materialization
  • 筆記具【ひっきぐ】– Writing instrument
真 (シン / ま) - True
  • 写真【しゃしん】– Photograph ("copy of truth")
  • 真実【しんじつ】– Truth, reality
  • 真ん中【まんなか】– Center, middle
  • 真夜中【まよなか】– Midnight
  • 真剣【しんけん】– Seriousness, earnest
工 (コウ / ク) - Craft
  • 工事【こうじ】– Construction work
  • 工場【こうじょう】– Factory
  • 工学【こうがく】– Engineering
  • 人工【じんこう】– Artificial, man-made
  • 大工【だいく】– Carpenter
