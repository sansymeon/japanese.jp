Compound Words – Lesson 3

舌 (ゼツ / した) - Tongue
  • 舌【した】– Tongue
  • 毒舌【どくぜつ】– Sharp tongue, sarcastic remark
  • 舌打ち【したうち】– Clicking one's tongue
  • 二枚舌【にまいじた】– Forked tongue; duplicity
  • 早口舌【はやくちした】– Fast-talking tongue
  • 甘舌【あまじた】– Sweet tongue (sweet talker)
升 (ショウ / ます) - Measuring Box
  • 升【ます】– Traditional measuring cup
  • 一升【いっしょう】– 1.8 liters (traditional measure)
  • 升酒【ますざけ】– Sake served in a wooden box
  • 升形【ますがた】– Square shape (like a measuring box)
  • 升目【ますめ】– Grid square (graph paper, shogi board)
  • 升売り【ますうり】– Selling by the measure
昇 (ショウ / のぼる) - Rise Up
  • 昇進【しょうしん】– Promotion (in rank/job)
  • 昇格【しょうかく】– Upgrading, promotion
  • 昇る【のぼる】– To rise (sun, smoke, etc.)
  • 上昇【じょうしょう】– Rise, increase (temperature, stock, etc.)
  • 昇天【しょうてん】– Ascension to heaven
  • 昇竜【しょうりゅう】– Rising dragon (common in game/fantasy context)
丸 (ガン / まる) - Round
  • 丸【まる】– Circle, round (Kunyomi)
  • 弾丸【だんがん】– Bullet
  • 丸薬【がんやく】– Pill, tablet
  • 日の丸【ひのまる】– Japan's national flag
  • 丸ごと【まるごと】– Whole, entirely
  • 丸太【まるた】– Log, timber
寸 (スン) – Measurement
  • 寸法【すんぽう】– Measurement, dimensions
  • 寸前【すんぜん】– Just before, on the verge of
  • 寸断【すんだん】– Cut into pieces
  • 寸劇【すんげき】– Short sketch or play
  • 寸志【すんし】– Small token of appreciation
  • 一寸【ちょっと】– Just a little (colloquial expression, also uses the kanji for “inch”)
肘 (チュウ / ひじ) – Elbow
  • 肘【ひじ】– Elbow (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 肘掛け【ひじかけ】– Armrest
  • 脱臼肘【だっきゅうひじ】– Dislocated elbow (used in medical contexts)
  • 肘鉄砲【ひじでっぽう】– Elbow jab (also used figuratively to mean a snub)
専 (セン) – Specialty
  • 専門【せんもん】– Specialty, field of expertise
  • 専用【せんよう】– Exclusive use
  • 専攻【せんこう】– Major (academic), subject of study
  • 専念【せんねん】– Devotion, dedication
  • 専制【せんせい】– Autocracy, despotism
  • 専売【せんばい】– Monopoly, exclusive sale
博 (ハク / バク) – Dr., Learned
  • 博士【はかせ／はくし】– Doctor (PhD), learned person
  • 博物館【はくぶつかん】– Museum
  • 博識【はくしき】– Erudition, wide knowledge
  • 博覧会【はくらんかい】– Expo, exhibition
  • 博打【ばくち】– Gambling
占 (セン / うらな・う、し・める) – Fortune-Telling
  • 占う【うらなう】– To tell a fortune, predict
  • 占い【うらない】– Fortune-telling
  • 独占【どくせん】– Monopoly, exclusive possession
  • 占有【せんゆう】– Possession, occupancy
  • 占拠【せんきょ】– Occupation (of territory, building, etc.)
  • 予占【よせん】– Prediction, forecast (less common term)
上 (ジョウ / うえ、あ・げる、あ・がる、のぼ・る) – Above
  • 上【うえ】– Above, up (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 上がる【あがる】– To rise, go up
  • 上げる【あげる】– To raise, lift up
  • 登上【とうじょう】– Ascend (formal/literary)
  • 向上【こうじょう】– Improvement, progress
  • 上手【じょうず】– Skillful, good at
下 (カ / ゲ / した、しも、さ・げる、さ・がる、くだ・る、くだ・さる、くだ・す、お・ろす、お・りる) – Below
  • 下【した】– Below, under (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 下がる【さがる】– To go down, drop
  • 下げる【さげる】– To lower, reduce
  • 地下【ちか】– Underground, basement
  • 低下【ていか】– Decline, deterioration
  • 下手【へた】– Unskilled, poor at
卓 (タク) – Eminent
  • 卓球【たっきゅう】– Table tennis, ping-pong
  • 卓上【たくじょう】– On the table, desktop
  • 卓越【たくえつ】– Excellence, superiority
  • 卓見【たっけん】– Insightful opinion, excellent view
  • 卓抜【たくばつ】– Preeminence, distinction
朝 (チョウ / あさ) – Morning
  • 朝【あさ】– Morning (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 今朝【けさ】– This morning
  • 毎朝【まいあさ】– Every morning
  • 朝食【ちょうしょく】– Breakfast
  • 朝日【あさひ】– Morning sun
  • 朝刊【ちょうかん】– Morning newspaper
嘲 (チョウ / あざけ・る) – Derision
  • 嘲る【あざける】– To ridicule, mock, sneer at
  • 冷嘲【れいちょう】– Sarcastic ridicule, cold sneer
  • 嘲笑【ちょうしょう】– Derisive laughter, scornful laugh
  • 嘲弄【ちょうろう】– Mockery, teasing
只 (シ / ただ) – Only
  • 只【ただ】– Only, just, merely (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 只今【ただいま】– Right now; I’m home! (greeting)
  • 無料【むりょう】– Free of charge (different kanji, but same concept)
  • 只管【ひたすら】– Earnestly, intently (rare usage)
  • 只一つ【ただひとつ】– The only one
  • 唯々諾々【いいだくだく】– Obediently, submissively (uses variant 唯)
貝 (バイ / かい) – Shellfish
  • 貝【かい】– Shellfish, shell (Kunyomi standalone)
  • 貝殻【かいがら】– Seashell
  • 巻貝【まきがい】– Spiral shell
  • 二枚貝【にまいがい】– Bivalve
  • 貝類【かいるい】– Shellfish (category)
  • 真珠貝【しんじゅがい】– Pearl oyster
唄 (バイ / うた) – Pop Song
  • 唄【うた】– Song, folk song (Kunyomi standalone; often written 歌)
  • 民謡唄【みんよううた】– Folk song
  • 演歌唄【えんかうた】– Enka song
  • 唄声【うたごえ】– Singing voice
  • 子守唄【こもりうた】– Lullaby
  • 祝い唄【いわいうた】– Celebration song
貞 (テイ / さだ) – Upright
  • 貞節【ていせつ】– Fidelity, chastity
  • 貞操【ていそう】– Virtue, moral integrity
  • 貞観【じょうがん】– Jōgan era (historical reference)
  • 貞明【ていめい】– Teimei (a Japanese empress; used in names)
  • 不貞【ふてい】– Infidelity, unfaithfulness
  • 貞女【ていじょ】– Chaste woman, virtuous lady
員 (イン) – Employee
  • 社員【しゃいん】– Company employee
  • 店員【てんいん】– Store clerk, shop staff
  • 職員【しょくいん】– Staff member, personnel
  • 全員【ぜんいん】– All members
  • 会員【かいいん】– Member (of a society or club)
  • 議員【ぎいん】– Assembly member, legislator
貼 (チョウ / は・る) – Post a Bill
  • 貼る【はる】– To stick, to paste, to post
  • 貼付【ちょうふ】– Attachment (of documents), affixing
  • 貼り紙【はりがみ】– Posted notice, paper sign
  • 値札を貼る【ねふだをはる】– To attach a price tag
  • 切手を貼る【きってをはる】– To affix a stamp
  • 掲示を貼る【けいじをはる】– To post a bulletin/notice
