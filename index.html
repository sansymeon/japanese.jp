Kanji Mnemonic Learning · KML

Learn Japanese Through Kanji, Vocabulary, Imagery and Music

KML is a growing Japanese learning curriculum combining kanji, high-frequency vocabulary, natural spoken Japanese, beautiful imagery, and peaceful music.

Learners can begin with elementary kanji, continue through compounds and post-elementary study, and gradually build practical spoken vocabulary.

The KML Journey

Kana → Elementary Kanji → Compounds → Post-Elementary Kanji → Advanced Compounds → Spoken Japanese

These projects are complementary layers of one curriculum — not unrelated video collections. Progress along the path that matches your level, or move between layers as your reading and speaking grow together.

Measurable curriculum

A Curriculum You Can Measure

KML tracks not only how many lessons have been created, but what learners actually encounter: vocabulary growth, kanji coverage, JLPT progression, spoken-frequency coverage, reinforcement, and exposure depth.

The homepage shows a few live headlines. The analytics dashboard is the deeper destination for charts, playlists, and search.
